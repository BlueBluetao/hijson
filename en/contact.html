Contact

HiJSON is maintained by one person. Email is the channel.

Email: develop_demo@163.com

Site: https://hijson.online

What to send

  • Parse failures — a public sample with tokens and phones removed
  • Feature requests (JSONPath gaps, large-file jank, import formats)
  • Guide corrections — which example does not run
  • Reuse / partnership — say how you will use it

Response time

Usually a few days. Not a ticket queue. If production is on fire, use a local tool first.

Do not send

Full production responses, personal data, or live secrets. Fake or masked fields are enough to talk about structure. See the privacy policy.
